Model information

Stage 1 – PCR / SVD: the 14 odds are standardised and compressed onto their first principal component (Z = UΣVᵀ, score t = Z·V₁, 57% of the variation). Goals are regressed on that one score and folded back into one equation on the raw odds: goals = b + A·odds.

Stage 2 – Handicap pick: the side with more predicted goals gets the 2-goal start: Home (2:0) if home ≥ away, otherwise Away (0:2). The pick wins unless that side loses by 2 or more.

Win chance: predicted goals are used as Poisson rates; the app adds up every score (0–10 each side) where the pick wins. This number runs cautious: it averaged about 90% while the picks actually won 97% in testing, so use it to rank matches, not as an exact chance.

Back-test: 25/26 on the training matches and 18/18 on 18 new matches (43/44). Always picking Home (2:0) won 41/44. Betting every PCR pick at the recorded handicap odds returned +6.5% on the 26 training matches and +8.0% on 7 new matches that had odds.

Important: trained on 26 matches only. Handicap odds are low (about 1.03–1.35), so one loss cancels several wins. Track results before staking real money.
