Model information

Stage 1: 14 decimal-odds inputs feed a two-output Ridge Regression model to estimate home and away goals.

Stage 2: Independent Poisson distributions convert those estimates into a scoreline grid from 0–0 to 8–8. The app displays only the single highest-probability score.

Training base: 26 historical matches. Ridge penalty α = 70.7946. This is a small experimental sample, not a validated or calibrated betting model.

Important: Do not interpret the top score probability as a reliable real-world chance. Validate on new matches before relying on the model.
